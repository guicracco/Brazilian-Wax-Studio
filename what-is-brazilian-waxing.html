WHAT IS BRAZILIAN WAXING?

A straightforward guide to the treatment, the appointment and deciding what feels right for you.

What does Brazilian waxing mean?

Brazilian waxing removes hair from the intimate area using warm wax. The exact finish can vary: some clients prefer most hair removed with a small amount left, while others choose a completely smooth finish. Names and preferences can differ, so Marcia will always confirm what you want before treatment begins.

What happens during an appointment?

First, Marcia discusses your preferences and answers any questions in a private room. She cleanses and prepares the skin, then applies wax in manageable sections. Strip-less hot wax is used for sensitive intimate zones. After removing the wax, she clears residue and applies a calming hydration lotion. You can ask for a pause at any time.

What are the benefits?

  • A smooth finish that typically lasts a few weeks, depending on your hair growth cycle.
  • Personal choice: you can discuss how much hair you want removed.
  • Regular waxing may lead to finer, slower regrowth for some people.
  • A discreet, professionally managed appointment with practical aftercare.

How should I prepare?

Hair around 0.5 cm (about a quarter of an inch) gives the wax a good grip. If it is much longer, trim beforehand; avoid shaving in the days before your appointment. Showering beforehand is recommended when possible, but coming directly from work is completely fine. The skin is cleansed before treatment.

What should I expect afterwards?

Mild redness is common and usually settles quickly. Avoid intense exercise, saunas and hot baths for 24 hours, and direct sun or sunbeds for 48 hours. Wear loose clothing and wait until the skin settles before gentle exfoliation. Marcia will explain aftercare for your skin and treatment.
