Male Waxing FAQs

Does male waxing hurt?

Waxing involves some discomfort, and sensitivity varies from person to person and from area to area. Intimate areas are more sensitive than the chest or legs. Marcia uses strip-less hot wax for sensitive intimate zones, and her technique and experience are focused on keeping the treatment as comfortable as possible. Tell her at any point if you need a moment.

How long should my hair be?

If your hair is long, trimming it to a short length before you arrive helps the wax grip properly and makes the treatment more comfortable. As a general guide, hair of roughly 0.5 cm (about a quarter of an inch) works well. Please do not shave in the days before your appointment. If you are unsure, ask Marcia when you book.

Is male intimate waxing available?

Yes. Marcia offers Full Male Brazilian, Full Male Hollywood, Male Colombian, Full Buttocks and Male Intimate treatments. Male waxing has been her speciality since 2008.

What is the difference between Brazilian and Hollywood?

In general, a Hollywood removes all hair from the intimate area, while a Brazilian can leave some hair in place if you prefer. Marcia will talk through exactly what you would like before starting. Prices: Full Male Brazilian £60.00, Full Male Hollywood £70.00.

How long does the appointment take?

The time depends on the treatment and the amount of hair. Marcia will give you an idea of timing when you book or at the start of your appointment, so there is no need to rush.

How long do results last?

This varies with each person's hair growth cycle, but results typically last a few weeks. Regular waxing usually leads to finer, slower regrowth over time. Marcia can advise on a sensible interval for you.

What happens after the wax?

Some redness is normal and usually settles quickly. A calming hydration lotion is applied at the end of the treatment, and Marcia will explain how to look after your skin.

Can I exercise afterwards?

Please avoid intense exercise for 24 hours after your appointment. Sweat and friction can irritate freshly waxed skin. Light activity is fine.

Can I swim afterwards?

Heat, chlorine and sun can irritate freshly waxed skin. It is best to wait before swimming; if you are unsure how long to leave it for the treatment you are having, ask Marcia and she will advise you.

What if it is my first time?

That is perfectly normal. You do not need to know the terminology or exactly which treatment to choose. Marcia will discuss the options and answer your questions before she starts. See our first-time guide.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Please mention it when you book or at the start of your appointment. The skin is cleansed and prepared before waxing to minimise sensitivity and reduce the risk of irritation, and Marcia can adapt her approach accordingly.
