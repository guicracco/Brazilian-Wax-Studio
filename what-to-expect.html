What to Expect from Male Waxing

Before, during and after your appointment — so there are no surprises.

A clear guide

What to Expect at Your Appointment

Knowing what happens, and in what order, takes the uncertainty out of a first visit.

Before your appointment

  • Hair length: around 0.5 cm (a quarter of an inch) works well.
  • Trimming: trim long hair beforehand; do not shave in the days before.
  • Showering: a shower is recommended where possible; coming straight from work is completely fine.
  • Exercise: avoid a heavy workout or a hot sauna just before.
  • Questions: ask Marcia anything when you book.

During your appointment

  • You are welcomed into a private, clean treatment room.
  • Marcia talks through what you would like done.
  • The skin is cleansed, then wax is applied; strip wax for body areas, strip-less hot wax for sensitive intimate zones.
  • Residue is removed and a calming hydration lotion is applied.
  • Duration depends on the treatment; Marcia will give you an idea at the start.

After your appointment

  • Redness: mild redness is normal and usually settles quickly.
  • Exercise: avoid intense exercise for 24 hours.
  • Swimming and heat: avoid saunas and hot baths for 24 hours, and sun and sunbeds for 48 hours.
  • Friction: loose, breathable clothing is more comfortable.
  • Exfoliation and ingrown hairs: exfoliate gently once the skin has settled.
Before you arrive

Come as you are

A shower beforehand is recommended whenever possible, but we understand many clients come straight from work — that is completely fine. Marcia will cleanse and prepare the skin before treatment. Avoid a heavy workout or hot sauna just beforehand.

Aftercare · including Luxury Back Waxing

Care for freshly waxed skin

After a back wax, keep the area cool and clean, wear loose clothing and avoid heat, intense exercise and friction for 24 hours. Once the skin has settled, gentle exfoliation can help reduce ingrown hairs. Marcia will tailor aftercare advice to your skin.

Intimate treatments

What clients can expect

Intimate treatments are carried out calmly, professionally and with privacy. Marcia has specialised in male waxing since 2008 and works in a matter-of-fact, reassuring way. You will be told what is happening at each stage, and you can ask questions or ask for a pause at any time.
