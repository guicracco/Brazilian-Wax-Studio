About the studio

Fourteen Years, One Craft: Marcia

Founder and master waxing specialist of Brazilian Waxing Studio London — trained in Brazil, and dedicated to a single discipline: precise, hygienic, comfortable male waxing.

Her story

Trained properly. Practised relentlessly.

Marcia's approach to waxing was shaped long before she opened her studio in London. Trained in Brazil as a master waxing specialist, she built her technique on a foundation of professional beauty and aesthetics education, with further specialist practice in intimate and body waxing.

In 2008, she brought that training to Central London and opened what became one of the city's pioneering dedicated Brazilian waxing studios — at a time when specialist, hygiene-first waxing studios were still rare in the capital. Since then, she has refined a signature technique built on preparation, precision, and aftercare, treating male clients from every walk of life across more than fourteen years.

Today, the studio remains proudly independent, small, and hands-on — every treatment is carried out personally by Marcia, a professional female therapist, in a private, spotlessly maintained room in Holborn.
