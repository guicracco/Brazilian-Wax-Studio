Her story

Trained properly. Practised relentlessly.

Marcia's approach to waxing was shaped long before she opened her studio in London. She trained at SENAC in Brazil, one of the country's most respected institutions for professional beauty and aesthetics education, before completing further specialist certification in intimate and body waxing at the Escola Técnica de Beleza in Portugal.

In 2008, she brought that training to Central London and opened what became one of the city's pioneering dedicated Brazilian waxing studios — at a time when specialist, hygiene-first waxing studios were still rare in the capital. Since then, she has refined a signature technique built on preparation, precision, and aftercare, treating clients from every walk of life across more than fourteen years.

Today, the studio remains proudly independent, small, and hands-on — every treatment is carried out personally by Marcia, in a private, spotlessly maintained room in Holborn.
