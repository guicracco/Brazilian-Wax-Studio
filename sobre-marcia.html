Her story

Trained properly. Practised relentlessly.

Marcia's approach to waxing was shaped long before she opened her studio in London. She trained at SENAC in Brazil, one of the country's most respected institutions for professional beauty and aesthetics education, before completing further specialist certification in intimate and body waxing at the Escola Técnica de Beleza in Portugal.

In 2008, she brought that training to Central London and opened what became one of the city's pioneering dedicated Brazilian waxing studios — at a time when specialist, hygiene-first waxing studios were still rare in the capital. Since then, she has refined a signature technique built on preparation, precision, and aftercare, treating clients from every walk of life across more than eighteen years.

Today, the studio remains proudly independent, small, and hands-on — every treatment is carried out personally by Marcia, in a private, spotlessly maintained room in Holborn.

My speciality

Male Waxing Has Been My Speciality Since 2008

I started working in London in 2008 as a mobile therapist, treating male clients. Over the years my work became more and more focused on male waxing, and I have since worked with many different skin types, hair types and sensitive areas.

I learned early that trust and comfort matter just as much as technique. Male waxing is not something I simply added to a list of beauty treatments. It is my speciality, and it still is today.
