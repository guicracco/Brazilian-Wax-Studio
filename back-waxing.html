Preparation and aftercare

Comfort before, care afterwards

A shower beforehand is recommended whenever possible, but coming straight from work is completely fine. After your wax, keep the skin cool and clean, avoid heat, intense exercise and friction for 24 hours, and wear loose clothing. Once the skin has settled, gentle exfoliation can help reduce ingrown hairs.

Marcia will tailor advice to your skin and the treatment. Visit our preparation and aftercare guide for more details.
