Our signature method

The 3-Step Waxing Technique

Every appointment follows the same disciplined process Marcia has refined over 14 years — built for hygiene, comfort, and lasting results.

1

Pre-Wax Cleansing & Preparation

Skin is thoroughly cleansed and soothed, and prepared for waxing to minimise sensitivity and reduce the risk of irritation.

2

Creamy Wax Application

Strip wax is used for body areas; strip-less hot wax is used for sensitive intimate zones, applied with precision and care.

3

Residue Removal & Hydration

All residue is carefully removed and a calming hydration lotion is applied to leave skin soft, soothed, and smooth.

Aftercare

Looking After Your Skin Post-Wax

Simple steps to keep your skin calm, smooth, and irritation-free after every appointment.

48h

Avoid UV & sunlight exposure

Freshly waxed skin is more sensitive to sunlight — avoid direct sun and sunbeds for 48 hours.

24h

Avoid saunas, hot baths & sunbeds

Heat can irritate freshly waxed skin — stick to lukewarm showers for the first 24 hours.

24h

Avoid intense exercise

Sweat and friction from intense workouts can irritate the skin — take it easy for a day.
